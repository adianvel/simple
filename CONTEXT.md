# Domain glossary

- **Portfolio**: A public professional profile that helps recruiters evaluate a candidate and freelance clients assess whether to start a conversation. It presents identity, capabilities, selected work, experience, and a contact path.

- **Writing**: Public writing and editorial features related to the portfolio owner. Each item identifies whether it is owner-authored or third-party coverage.
- **Media feature**: Third-party coverage about or including the portfolio owner; its publisher and reporter retain attribution.
- **Tools I work with**: The factual list of technologies the portfolio owner uses, shown in the Hero. Every entry is a claim about the owner's stack.
- **Decorative icon pile**: The footer's cluster of third-party logos. It is purely ornamental: it makes no claim that the owner uses those products, carries no labels, links, or tooltips, and is hidden from assistive technology. It deliberately mixes developer tools with widely recognized consumer brands.
